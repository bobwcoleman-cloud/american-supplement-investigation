Install SuppleFacts on your phone

No App Store and no download. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens full-screen like any other app.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open supplements.brokenpromiseshealthcare.org/supplefacts.html in Safari (scan the code below with your camera).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen, then scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The SuppleFacts leaf icon is now on your Home Screen.

Using Chrome on iPhone? Tap the Share button in its address bar and choose Add to Home Screen. If you don’t see the option, open the page in Safari instead.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install.
