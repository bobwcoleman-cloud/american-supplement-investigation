About this data: Label information is sourced directly from the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements' Dietary Supplement Label Database (DSLD), drawn from over 200,000 product labels. Submission is voluntary and manufacturer-reported — the NIH itself notes that labels "might be incomplete and/or inaccurate," and inclusion in the database is not an endorsement of the product by NIH, FDA, or HHS. This tool shows you what a label declares; it does not carry this site's own evidence ratings, which you'll find on the Top 25 guide for the most common ingredients.

Data: National Institutes of Health, Office of Dietary Supplements — Dietary Supplement Label Database (dsld.od.nih.gov). Public domain (CC0 1.0).
