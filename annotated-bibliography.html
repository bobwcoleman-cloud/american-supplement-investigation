The central case study of Book One. Found an 18% increase in lung cancer diagnoses and an 8% increase in deaths among smokers given beta-carotene — the opposite of the hypothesized protective effect.

Research Report

Protein Powders and Shakes Contain High Levels of Lead

Martineau, Paris · Consumer Reports · October 2025 (updated January 2026)

Tested 23 protein powders and shakes; more than two-thirds exceeded CR's 0.5-microgram lead benchmark per serving. Naked Nutrition's Vegan Mass Gainer and Huel's Black Edition were highest, at roughly 1,570% and 1,290% of that benchmark.

Cited in: Heavy Metals in Protein Powder, Top 25, FAQ

Peer-Reviewed Study

Quantifying Lead and Cadmium in Protein Supplements: Insights Into Dietary Exposure and Health Risks

Bethencourt-Barbuzano, Elena, et al. · Journal of Food Composition and Analysis · 2025

Tested 90 European protein supplements using validated lab methods. Animal-based products had slightly higher mean lead; plant-based had slightly higher mean cadmium — neither difference statistically significant. No health risk found at a typical 30g/day serving.
